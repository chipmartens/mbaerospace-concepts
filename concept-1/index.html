Manitoba is organising for defence.

Government, industry, colleges, universities and the Canadian Armed Forces now work as one alliance, with a dedicated defence lead based with us. Here is 2026 so far.

  1. April 24, 2026$19.5M in federal defence investment for three Winnipeg projects, including Magellan and StandardAero. More than 150 jobs created.
  2. September 8, 2026The Manitoba-Canada Defence Alliance launches, with a defence lead based at Manitoba Aerospace & Defence.
  3. September 16, 2026Over $3.8M for the Defence and Dual-Use Innovation Bridge, to bring 100 Manitoba SMEs into defence supply chains.
  4. September 28, 2026UK delegates tour Boeing, StandardAero and Magellan at the first Manitoba-UK Defence Summit.

More than engines.

Composites, rockets and the maintenance behind every flight hour.

Composites

Boeing Winnipeg is Canada's largest aerospace composite plant, building here since 1971.

Rockets and satellites

Magellan builds Black Brant sounding rockets and satellite buses in Winnipeg.

Maintenance and support

KF Aerospace has supported DND programs for more than 25 years. PAL Aerospace adds ISR, in-service support and aircrew training.
