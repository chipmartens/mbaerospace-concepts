Imagery recipe: Night ramp

  1. Whole frame, one treatment. Saturation to 42 percent across everything, so each photo keeps its own temperature. No subject left in colour.
  2. Texture kept: lifted blacks, gentle S-curve, local contrast, heavier grain in the shadows. Night skies lift toward the horizon, so aircraft sit in the scene.
  3. Steel only in the shadows; mids neutral grey, so airframes read as metal, not lavender.
  4. Practical light stays warm: apron floods, beacons, sodium lamps, pilot lights.
  5. Subjects: aircraft at night, at dusk and in winter, shown working, and the people who build them.
