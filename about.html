About these concepts

Three early directions for a new Manitoba Aerospace & Defence logo and homepage. They show what's possible. They don't lock anything in.

Each one makes the same case from a different angle: Manitoba is Canada's third-largest aerospace cluster, and the industry that builds, overhauls, tests and trains for Canada's air force is already here. Switch between the homepage and the logo for each concept with the toggle at the bottom left.

The finished brand and site come out of a full strategy and creative deep dive with your team and members. That's where we agree who the site is for, what it says first, and how to show your members' real work.

What's real and what's placeholder

  • Facts, figures, members and events come from your published information and government releases. Some figures are dated where they are your own earlier numbers.
  • The supplier search and forms run on sample data. Nothing is sent.
  • Photography is licensed stand-in imagery. Photos of your members' people, shops and aircraft replace it.
  • The name "Manitoba Aerospace & Defence" follows your new brand direction.

Credits

  • Stand-in photography: US Air Force, NASA and other public domain sources, and Wikimedia Commons contributors under Creative Commons licences. Full credits are on each concept's credits page.
  • Type: Switzer, Archivo, Manrope and Source Serif, with your device's system font.
